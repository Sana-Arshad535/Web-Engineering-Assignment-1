Welcome to ChowChowCrochet


We are open!

Chow Chow Crochet is a small handmade crochet studio that specializes in creating soft, fluffy, and irresistibly cute crochet hair clips. Inspired by the cozy and cuddly nature of the Chow Chow, every piece is carefully crafted stitch by stitch using quality yarn and a whole lot of love. Our clips are designed to add a playful, handmade touch to your everyday style — whether you’re keeping it simple or dressing up for something special.

We believe that handmade accessories are more than just fashion pieces. Each item takes time, patience, and creativity to make, which gives it a special charm that mass-produced accessories often do not have. Our crochet products are made in small batches so that we can pay attention to every little detail and maintain good quality.

From colorful flower clips to soft hair bands and other cute accessories, our designs are made for people who love simple, cozy, and unique styles. We enjoy experimenting with different colors, patterns, and designs to create accessories that are both fun and easy to wear. Each clip is unique, small-batch, and made to bring a little warmth and joy to your look.

We also love creating new designs based on different seasons, trends, and customer preferences. Whether you are looking for something cute for yourself or a small handmade gift for someone special, Chow Chow Crochet aims to make every piece feel personal and meaningful.
Follow along for new designs, and DM us to order your new favorite accessory. 🧶✨
